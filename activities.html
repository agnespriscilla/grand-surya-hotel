ACTIVITIES AT KEDIRI

Activities

BICYCLE

We provide you Bike for Rent, it might be useful to explore.

SIMPANG LIMA GUMUL

Built in 2003, Monument Simpang Lima Gumul is a famous monument in Kediri located at the road junction in Desa Tugurejo, Kecamatan Gampengrejo.

WATERFALL AT BESUKI

Beautiful waterfall in Besuki, Desa Jugo. Located at 25km away from the city Kediri, this natural waterfall is not one to miss.

RED CHURCH

GPB Immanuel building or well known as the Red Church was founded in 1904. This is the oldest church in Kediri and was designed by J.A. Boers who was also a pastor at that time.

SURAWONO TEMPLE

Situated at Desa Canggu (Pare City). Surawono Temple is a great heritage from Majapahit era that showcases the rich history of King Wengker.

TEGOWANGI TEMPLE

Tegowangi Temple is a funerary of King Bhre Matahun built in 1385. Decorated with beautiful carvings which narrates the story of Wayang Purwo.

MARIA LOURDES CAVE

Also known as "Puhsarang", is the oldest Catholic Church cave in Southeast Asia.

KELUD MOUNTAIN

Kelud Mountain is an active volcanic mountain situated at 27km away from Kota Kediri. A great place for sightseeing, camping and adventure.

SELOMANGLENG CAVE

Selomangleng Cave used to be used by Dewi Kilisuci as a hermitage. She refused to accept the throne and preferred to distance herself from the world by meditating here.

Tjoe Hwie Kiong Tjoe Hwie Kiong

Tjoe Hwie Kiong Tjoe Hwie Kiong is already known as the oldest temple in the city of Kediri. When you enter the temple courtyard, you will be entertained by a statue of Admiral Zheng He who stands firmly with dragon carvings in the form of a book at the base. So there’s no doubt about the beauty and authenticity

Nasi Pecel

Nasi Pecel of Jalan Dhoho Pecel Tumpang is one of the typical culinary icon Kediri. NASI PECEL is rice served with mix steamed vegetables with peanut sauce and soybean sauce served with various side dishes such as crackers, fried tofu & fried soybean and other side dishes. Only by walking to the front of Grand Surya Hotel along Dhoho road after sunset you can find dozens of sellers NASI PECEL TUMPANG. For those of you who do not like soybean sauce you can request rice with peanut sauce only.
